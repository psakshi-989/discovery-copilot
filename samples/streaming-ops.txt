Priya (Head of Content Operations, Lumen Streaming): Thanks for making time. Quick background: we run a regional streaming service, about 4,000 titles, and every title needs artwork and metadata localised into six languages before it goes live.

Marco: Where does the time go?

Priya: Mostly waiting. Artwork requests sit in a shared inbox. And localisation vendors send back files in different formats.

Priya: Around 150 new titles a month, plus roughly 400 metadata corrections.
